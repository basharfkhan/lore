How it works

  1. It thinks about failureAn AI agent reads your brief and works out the distinct ways the system could fail: design, software, testing, handling, schedule pressure.
  2. It searches by mechanismIt searches the archive once per failure mode, then reads the promising documents in full. The best precedent is usually about different hardware.
  3. It shows its sourcesEvery link in the answer is checked against the documents the agent actually retrieved. Anything it could not have read is flagged.
