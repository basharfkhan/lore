LORELessons and Outcomes Retrieval Engine

Describe a spacecraft system, an operation or a design decision. LORE searches NASA's lessons-learned archive and mishap investigation reports for failures of the same kind, and tells you what happened and what the investigators recommended.

How it works

  • An AI agent (Claude) reads your description, thinks of the ways it could fail, and searches the archive once per failure mode. It reads the promising documents in full before writing.
  • The archive is 2,117 NASA lessons (1972 to 2025) and 17 mishap investigation reports from Apollo 1 to Glory, about 1,900 pages, some recovered by OCR from scans.
  • Every link in an answer is checked against the documents the agent actually retrieved in that run. Anything it could not have read is flagged.

Does searching by failure mode help?

Tested on real incidents the model does not recognise, using only documents written before each incident. The question is whether a lesson describing the same failure mechanism appears in the top 30 results.
